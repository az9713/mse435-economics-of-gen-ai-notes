How to study this edition

Read Chapters 1–3 for accounting, compute, and physical infrastructure. Chapters 4–6 explain organizational adoption and model specialization. Chapters 7–9 apply those ideas to software, inference services, and biology. Algebra and basic probability suffice for most sections; calculus, optimization, and thermodynamics appear where they clarify a mechanism.

Start a section from its timestamp to hear the source discussion, then work through the added model and its assumptions. Attempt the exercises before reading each chapter's solutions. The lecture map makes source navigation explicit; the reference library collects the primary sources.

Attribution and evidence boundaries

These are independent study notes based on the nine-recording playlist for Stanford MS&E435: Economics of the AI Supercycle, Spring 2026. They are not official Stanford notes and imply no endorsement by Stanford or the speakers.

The complete English caption tracks were acquired and read for all nine recordings. YouTube's caption API labels each selected track as not automatically generated; that label does not establish verbatim accuracy or editorial quality. Names and technical terms were checked where possible against primary sources. The source review was caption-based: unseen slide details and visual demonstrations are not claimed as independently inspected.

Speaker accounts are attributed in the prose. Definitions, worked examples, code, and mathematical derivations are independent teaching additions unless explicitly stated otherwise. Research extensions cite scholarship or official documentation beside the claims they support. Speaker forecasts, company statistics, and conversational estimates remain attributed rather than becoming verified current market facts.

A timestamp links to a relevant discussion, not proof of every sentence beneath its heading. Sections sometimes revisit earlier material to group related arguments; the source map preserves the actual recording times. The link count measures navigation coverage, not the fraction of original prose or mathematics derived from a lecture. Each chapter states its coverage limits.

Editorial approach

The transcript supplies the chapter's structure. Our original definitions, derivations, examples, executable models, research extensions, exercises and solutions develop it into a self-contained textbook. These additions are distinguished from the recorded discussion.

Files and reproducibility
